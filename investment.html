Investment FAQ

Which property type is best for investment?

There is no universal best type. Suitability depends on budget, horizon, risk tolerance and management preference.

Does Landline guarantee returns?

No. Property values and income can rise or fall, and all estimates require independent verification.

What due diligence should I complete?

Review title, approvals, permitted use, RERA information where applicable, condition and transaction terms.

How should I compare locations?

Consider connectivity, employment access, supply, end-user demand and the exact micro-market.

Is the calculator a forecast?

No. It only demonstrates compound growth using the assumptions you enter.

Can I arrange a site visit?

Yes. Enquire on an opportunity to coordinate a suitable time.
