New Project FAQ

What is considered a new launch?

A project recently introduced to the market, subject to applicable approvals and disclosures.

How do I verify a project's RERA information?

Use the relevant state RERA authority and match the official record with supplied documents.

Should I visit an under-construction project?

Yes. Inspect the site and surrounding area, while respecting site safety and access rules.

Are project prices shown here final?

No. Generic demo entries use price on request; current commercial terms require confirmation.

What should I compare between projects?

Compare location, configuration, approvals, status, amenities, payment terms and maintenance expectations.

Can Landline arrange a project visit?

Yes. Request details and provide your preferred timing.
